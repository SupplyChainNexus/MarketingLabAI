## MLAI-032.1 — Marketing Efficiency Measurement Foundation

### Purpose

Implement the evidence model and instrumentation required to evaluate the
locked time-saving target honestly, without collecting real-customer data or
claiming that synthetic benchmarks prove customer outcomes.

### Acceptance

- Versioned workflow definitions establish equivalent manual and assisted
  boundaries.
- Append-only measurement records retain active time, elapsed time, reviews,
  revisions, exceptions, compliance findings and acceptance quality.
- Synthetic benchmark data is unmistakably classified and excluded from
  validated customer claims.
- Reports state sample, role, business type, period, mean, median and range.
- Ordinary Company Brain workflow designs target no more than three meaningful
  operator decisions and justify every exception.
- No public claim is promoted automatically by test or benchmark success.
- Real-customer measurement remains separately authorized.
