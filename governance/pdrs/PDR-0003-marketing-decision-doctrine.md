# PDR-0003 — Marketing Decision Doctrine

## Status

Accepted

## Date

2026-08-05

## Decision owner

Founder

## Context

MarketingLabAI has established governed Company, Customer, Product, Campaign,
Brief, Generation, Compliance, Identity, API, and Workspace capabilities. A
review against foundational marketing principles found that the platform is
strong in evidence integrity, controlled execution, security, compliance, and
auditability, while full Positioning, Strategy, environmental scanning,
measurement, and Learning Intelligence remain intentionally incomplete.

Without a durable marketing decision doctrine, future implementation could
mistake governed content generation for a complete marketing operating system,
or encode familiar frameworks as disconnected questionnaires rather than
evidence-backed decisions.

## Rabbit Rule classification

Core.

## Decision

MarketingLabAI will use this canonical marketing decision loop:

```text
Verified company and market evidence
    -> Customer segmentation
    -> Target selection
    -> Positioning
    -> Marketing-mix decisions
    -> Objectives and strategy
    -> Campaign planning and approved briefs
    -> Governed generation
    -> Independent compliance
    -> Controlled execution
    -> Measurement
    -> Evidence-backed learning and adaptation
```

The following laws govern the loop:

1. Customer value leads every marketing decision.
2. Marketing decisions connect to an explicit business objective.
3. Research evidence, assumptions, uncertainty, and unknowns remain distinct.
4. Segmentation and target selection precede final positioning and strategy.
5. Product, Price, Place, and Promotion form the minimum marketing-mix lens.
6. Service contexts may add People, Process, and Physical Evidence.
7. Environmental analysis covers at least Political, Economic, Social, and
   Technological factors and may extend to Legal and Environmental factors.
8. Marketing-mix elements are evaluated as a coherent system rather than
   independent fields.
9. A measurable objective identifies a target, method, timeframe, and eventual
   actual result.
10. Learning and optimization claims require real outcome evidence.
11. AI may assist analysis but may not silently invent research, positioning,
    strategy, performance, or learning.
12. Humans retain approval authority at governed decision and execution gates.

These frameworks are required reasoning coverage where relevant, not one rigid
questionnaire for every campaign. A workflow may mark a factor irrelevant or
unknown, but it may not silently omit a material factor.

## Relationship to existing architecture

This decision strengthens and does not replace ADR-0004 or LDR-013 through
LDR-018. The established intelligence hierarchy remains the implementation
dependency order. Prompt builders remain renderers, verified evidence remains
preferred, and learning remains downstream of real execution.

MLAI-027.6 remains an operational release story. It must not absorb full
Positioning, Strategy, environmental-intelligence, Analytics, or Learning
engines. Those capabilities follow the pilot gate and must be delivered in
dependency order.

## Capability interpretation

- Company, Customer, and Product Intelligence supply verified decision inputs.
- Positioning Intelligence owns target-product relevance, differentiation,
  value proposition, alternatives, and supporting proof.
- Marketing Strategy Intelligence owns situation synthesis, objectives,
  coherent marketing-mix choices, channel roles, and measurement plans.
- Campaign Plans and Marketing Briefs translate approved strategy into
  controlled work; they do not replace strategy.
- Compliance independently evaluates generated output and proposed execution.
- Learning Intelligence records actual outcomes and approved lessons only when
  real evidence exists.

## Alternatives considered

### Treat the reference frameworks as optional guidance

Rejected because future engineers could preserve technically correct
generation while losing the marketing discipline that defines the product.

### Add every framework field to the MLAI-027.6 workspace

Rejected because that would mix strategic-domain expansion with operational
release work and create form fields without the required domain lifecycle.

### Make one framework mandatory for every use case

Rejected because product, service, market, and campaign contexts differ. The
system requires explicit decision coverage, evidence, and omissions—not
ceremonial completion of irrelevant fields.

## Consequences

- Future Positioning and Strategy work receives an explicit product contract.
- Capability reviews can distinguish input data, decisions, execution, and
  learning.
- Pilot outputs must not be presented as full strategic intelligence.
- Measurement plans remain useful, but performance claims remain prohibited
  until actual results are captured.
- MLAI-027.6 retains its security and operations scope.

## Superseded decisions

None. This decision clarifies and extends the accepted intelligence hierarchy.
