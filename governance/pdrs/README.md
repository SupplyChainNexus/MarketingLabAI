# Product Decision Records

PDRs preserve durable product and commercial decisions. Use an ADR for a purely
technical decision and a PDR when the decision changes product identity,
customer promise, audience, packaging law, scope, or strategic sequencing.

PDR filenames use `PDR-NNNN-short-title.md`. Never reuse a number. Accepted PDRs
must identify superseded decisions and update the locked-decision register.
