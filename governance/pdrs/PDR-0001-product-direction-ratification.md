# PDR-0001 — Product Direction Ratification

## Status

Accepted

## Date

2026-08-05

## Decision owner

Founder

## Context

A long-running product and engineering conversation contained important product
direction, commercial ideas, architectural principles, and future hypotheses.
The repository continuity work recovered those decisions, reconciled them with
tested implementation and accepted ADRs, and identified which decisions should
be permanent versus evidence-dependent.

The founder reviewed the recommendations and approved all of them on
2026-08-05.

## Rabbit Rule classification

Core governance decision.

## Decision

### Permanently ratified product direction

- MarketingLabAI is a Marketing Intelligence Operating System.
- Its customer promise is an AI Marketing Department for growing businesses.
- AI is the engine, not the headline; customer outcomes lead product language.
- The product is not merely an AI writer, generic assistant, CRM, ERP,
  accounting platform, or core Executive Operating System.
- Executive Intelligence remains a future premium extension and may not
  redirect the marketing core.
- Proposals must be classified using the Rabbit Rule: Core, Future, or Rabbit.

### Permanently ratified engineering principles

- Deterministic-first reasoning and explicit missing context
- Evidence-grounded learning; no synthetic assumptions represented as learning
- The accepted Marketing Intelligence hierarchy
- Company Brain as governed organizational knowledge authority
- Prompt builders as renderers rather than owners of business reasoning
- Provider neutrality and connector isolation
- Tenant isolation, least privilege, auditability, and human accountability
- Separate domain lifecycles and explicit dependency direction
- Generator-level permanent corrections instead of recurring package repairs
- Focused and complete regression validation
- Buyer-ready documentation, decision records, risk, debt, and handover evidence

### Subscription philosophy

Every tier must provide a complete, trustworthy experience for its intended
customer. Correctness, core professional quality, responsible compliance,
security, evidence integrity, and honest limitations may not be intentionally
degraded to force upgrades.

Higher tiers may add capacity, brands, users, collaboration, storage,
integration breadth, automation frequency, analytics depth, governance, and
organizational complexity.

Customers should upgrade because their needs and organizations have grown, not
because a lower tier was deliberately made inadequate.

### Provisional tier names

`Launch / Growth / Scale / Enterprise` is the approved working naming system.
It is not permanently locked and may change when packaging and customer
research provide stronger evidence. `Executive` is not used as a tier name to
avoid confusion with the deferred Executive Intelligence layer.

### Founding pricing hypothesis

`R399 / R999 / R2,499 / Custom` is preserved as Founding Pricing Hypothesis v1.
It is not approved active pricing and must not be published or encoded as a
commercial commitment before unit economics, taxes, provider costs, fair-use
limits, support obligations, packaging, and market validation are reviewed.

### Marketing Intelligence Score

The Marketing Intelligence Score remains a protected future hypothesis. It may
not be represented as an objective measure until its evidence model, weighting,
explainability, uncertainty, validation, and anti-gaming controls are defined.
An early implementation should behave as a transparent readiness assessment.

### Next engineering-selection rule

MLAI-026 is technically unblocked but is not automatically authorized as the
next epic. A Launch Readiness and Vertical-Slice Review must first identify the
largest obstacle preventing a real customer from completing an end-to-end
MarketingLabAI journey. The review may select MLAI-026 when evidence shows that
Marketing Calendar is the correct next investment.

## Alternatives considered

### Lock every recovered statement permanently

Rejected because it would give provisional prices, packaging names, old sprint
numbers, and unvalidated product hypotheses the same authority as enduring
product and engineering principles.

### Leave all recovered decisions open

Rejected because it would allow future implementation to drift from the
founder's established product direction.

### Start MLAI-026 automatically

Rejected because technical dependency order alone does not establish launch or
commercial priority.

## Consequences

- Future engineers receive a clear, founder-approved product direction.
- Commercial learning remains possible without weakening the constitution.
- Product hypotheses cannot be mistaken for implemented or validated facts.
- The next epic must be selected from end-to-end customer value evidence.
- Material changes require explicit PDR or ADR supersession.
