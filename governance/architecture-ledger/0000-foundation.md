## Objective

Introduce permanent engineering governance to MarketingLabAI.

The objective of this milestone is not to add product functionality, but to establish a professional engineering operating model that will scale with the platform as it grows.

This governance foundation supports maintainability, architectural consistency, knowledge preservation, engineering quality, and long-term due diligence readiness.

## Engineering Principles Established

This milestone formally adopts the following permanent principles.

- Documentation Discovery before Architecture Discovery.
- Architecture before implementation.
- Extend before replacing.
- Preserve architectural history.
- Every significant engineering decision should be explainable.
- Documentation is part of the product.
- Build buyer-ready by default.

---

## Architectural Impact

MarketingLabAI now separates concerns between:

- Product documentation
- Architecture documentation
- Engineering governance
- Technical registers
- Historical architecture decisions

This creates a scalable engineering process suitable for long-term development.
