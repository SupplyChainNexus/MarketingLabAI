# ADR-0036 — Simple Operator Experience and Measured Marketing Efficiency

## Status

Accepted by the founder on 2026-08-10. Implementation is queued behind the
active controlled-hosting and zero-trust supply-chain storyline.

## Context

MarketingLabAI's governance, provenance and intelligence architecture is
necessarily rigorous. Exposing those mechanics directly would transfer system
complexity to non-technical operators. Separately, time-saving claims are common
in marketing software but are often based on narrow tasks, selected examples or
large enterprise totals that do not establish a typical small-business result.

## Decision

Governance complexity is absorbed by the platform. Operators interact with
business decisions and genuine exceptions. An ordinary Company Brain task
targets no more than three meaningful decisions. Additional actions require a
recorded conflict, risk, approval separation or recovery need. Administrator
and audit evidence uses progressive disclosure.

MarketingLabAI adopts an initial measurable target of 5-10 hours saved per week
and a 40-60% reduction in repeatable campaign-preparation time for growing
businesses and lean marketing teams. Until representative customer-pilot
evidence validates the target, external language must say "designed to", "aims
to", or "targeting". The platform must not claim a guarantee, a proven average,
or replacement of a complete marketing department.

Equivalent manual and assisted workflows must measure total operator minutes,
review time, revision cycles, missing-context resolution, compliance findings
and acceptance quality. Results identify sample size, business type, operator
role, period, mean, median and range. Synthetic benchmarks remain synthetic and
cannot become customer evidence or organizational learning.

## Consequences

- Product simplicity becomes an architectural requirement rather than visual
  polish applied after implementation.
- Commercial claims remain honest while the product builds its evidence base.
- Measurement instrumentation must precede validated time-saving claims.
- Exceptional high-risk workflows may exceed three decisions when the reason is
  explicit and tested.
- Customer outcome measurement is queued without interrupting active hosting.

## Validation

Governance tests require the exact target, qualification boundary, progressive
disclosure rule, three-decision target and evidence categories. CI executes the
PowerShell 5.1-compatible validator. Later stories must add workflow telemetry,
synthetic baselines and separately authorized customer-pilot evidence.
