# ADR-0029 — Controlled Real-Data Activation

## Decision

Velani Wholesale is the first activation candidate because an independent
operator provides customer-experience evidence that founder-operated use cannot.
Selection does not activate the tenant.

Activation is two-stage. Stage one permits one approved external Velani operator
to complete the full experience using synthetic content. Stage two may permit
only enumerated, founder-approved company and product categories after current
partner evidence passes. Decisions are tenant-, environment-, commit-, founder-,
scope- and time-bound, append-only, and immediately suspendable.

Customer, employee, supplier and transaction records; credentials; public
signup; billing; direct publishing; cross-tenant reuse; and real-data model
learning remain prohibited. Strand Auto Parts remains independently frozen.

## Consequences

Readiness never self-authorizes activation. A current MLAI-030.6 assessment and
an explicit founder decision are both required. Product feedback may guide
governed engineering but submitted content may not train models.
