# MarketingLabAI Intelligence Roadmap

## Product Direction

MarketingLabAI is evolving from governed campaign generation into a Marketing Intelligence Operating System (MIOS).

Its purpose is not simply to generate marketing content, but to understand a business, reason about its customers and products, develop strategy, generate governed campaigns, learn from outcomes, and support executive decision-making.

This roadmap defines the long-term evolution of the platform and the order in which intelligence capabilities should be built so that higher-level decisions remain grounded in validated lower-level context.

## Marketing Intelligence Pyramid

```text
Executive Intelligence
        ↑
Learning and Analytics Intelligence
        ↑
Campaign Intelligence and Execution
        ↑
Marketing Strategy Intelligence
        ↑
Positioning Intelligence
        ↑
Product Intelligence
        ↑
Customer Intelligence
        ↑
Company Intelligence
```

Each higher intelligence layer should consume the strongest validated context available from the layers beneath it.

Incomplete lower-layer information may be tolerated during progressive onboarding, but missing information must never be silently invented or represented as verified Company Brain knowledge.

# Sequencing Rules

1. Customer Intelligence precedes Product Positioning.
2. Product Intelligence precedes Positioning Intelligence.
3. Positioning Intelligence precedes Marketing Strategy Intelligence.
4. Strategy should consume available Company, Customer, Product, and Positioning context.
5. Generation should use the strongest verified strategic context available.
6. Compliance guidance does not replace post-generation validation.
7. Learning requires real execution evidence.
8. Forecasting and optimisation require sufficient historical data.
9. Missing information must remain explicit.
10. AI-generated assumptions must not become verified Company Brain knowledge without review and approval.

# Engineering Principles

The roadmap follows these principles:

1. Intelligence before generation.
2. Deterministic reasoning before AI reasoning.
3. Verified business knowledge before assumptions.
4. Prompt builders remain presentation adapters.
5. Human governance remains above automated execution.
6. Learning must come from evidence, not speculation.
7. Every intelligence layer must strengthen the platform.
8. New capabilities must preserve tenant, brand, audit, and compliance boundaries.
9. Material architectural changes require an ADR.
10. Capability maturity must be reviewed after major increments.

# Roadmap Governance

After every major capability increment:

- Update the Product Capability Map
- Review capability maturity
- Update relevant ADRs
- Record accepted risks
- Record unresolved technical debt
- Confirm the next dependency is genuinely ready
- Ensure implemented behaviour matches this roadmap
- Ensure automated tests cover the affected intelligence and workflow paths

# Long-Term Product Outcome

MarketingLabAI should evolve into a platform that can:

1. Understand the organisation.
2. Understand its customers.
3. Understand its products.
4. Select relevant positioning.
5. Develop evidence-grounded strategy.
6. Generate governed campaign assets.
7. Validate outputs independently.
8. Observe actual outcomes.
9. Learn from evidence.
10. Support increasingly valuable marketing decisions.

The platform's destination is not automated content production alone.

Its destination is governed, explainable, and continuously improving marketing intelligence.
