# Marketing Efficiency Evidence Protocol

## Locked target

MarketingLabAI targets 5-10 hours saved per week and a 40-60% reduction in
repeatable campaign-preparation time. The target is not a proven claim.

## Evidence levels

1. **Target** — use `designed to`, `aims to`, or `targeting`.
2. **Synthetic benchmark** — publish controlled task comparisons labelled as
   synthetic.
3. **Customer-pilot observation** — report the measured pilot, sample and
   limitations without generalising beyond it.
4. **Validated commercial claim** — permitted only after representative,
   repeatable evidence and governance approval.

## Canonical workflow measurements

- context gathering;
- campaign-plan preparation;
- marketing-brief preparation;
- multi-channel first-draft preparation;
- rules, claims and disclaimer review; and
- reuse of approved decisions and learning evidence.

Each measurement records the same start state, deliverable, quality bar and
human-approval boundary for manual and assisted work. Capture elapsed time,
operator-active time, review time, revisions, exceptions, compliance findings
and whether the result was accepted with minor, major or no edits.

## Reporting

Every report names the run, workflow version, product version, business type,
operator role, sample size and period. Report mean, median and range. Failed and
abandoned runs remain in the evidence set with a classification. Never select
only the fastest result.

## Prohibited transformations

- Synthetic evidence cannot become a customer testimonial.
- One customer's saving cannot become a typical-customer claim.
- Team-wide totals cannot be represented as per-user savings.
- Task-specific percentages cannot be represented as whole-business savings.
- Time saved cannot be represented as revenue, campaign performance or legal
  compliance without separate evidence.
